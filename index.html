DIU mail agent

A personal tool that reads incoming Gmail messages, drafts replies with the help of an AI model, and sends them on the owner's behalf.

What it does

  • Reads messages in the owner's own Gmail inbox.
  • Drafts suggested replies.
  • Sends or labels messages from the owner's own account.

Who can use it

This app is built for personal use by its developer. It is not offered to the public, and it only ever accesses the Google account of the person who signs in to it.
